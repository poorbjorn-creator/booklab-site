Levels of Energy

A map of consciousness from 0 to 1000 — half of you will love it, half of you will hate it. That's kind of the point.

by Frederick Dodson

What's It About?

Here is something they didn't teach you in school. The premise of Levels of Energy is that there are clearly defined levels of consciousness. People experience reality differently — and act and think in certain ways — depending on their energy "level." Dodson lays out his whole spectral energy scale, from 0 to 1000, from the hellish to joy and bliss. And it's quite a ride.

A very simplified version of the scale:

Your baseline is a home frequency. Events are weather. Move step by step - and pick the club that fits the shot.

🔥 0-100: The Hellish
Victimhood, guilt, shame, apathy, grief. Past-heavy, low power, often numb or crushed. Desire and simple entertainment can be a step up from here - not a moral failure.
😤 100-199: Low & Animalistic
Fear, neediness, anger, antagonism, pride. More active than the hellish band, still stuck in having or not having. Anger can liberate fear; pride is the ceiling of the low band.
⚙️ 200-299: Function & Physicality
Routine, contentment, hard work, then courage and fun. Life becomes doable. TV, idols, and "good enough" live here. Purpose starts to matter. Act before 275 slides back into flatness.
🚀 300s: Success & Productivity
Willingness, kindness, optimism, self-motivation. Yes to life. Strong band for building, shipping, and lifting others without living in permanent bliss.
🧠 400s: Intellectual
Attention, neutrality, reason. Researchers and explorers. Bookstore-as-shrine energy. Overthinking is the trap that blocks the climb into joy.
💛 500s: Joy & Love
Creativity, appreciation, initiative, love, humor, exaltation. Less forcing; more interesting than important. Follow your bliss. Rare as a full-time home.
✨ 600-1000: Peace & Bliss
Serenity and beyond. Use the map; don't worship the number. Match the level to the task.

Dodson's bigger frame: lower bands lean toward having, mid bands toward doing, high bands toward being. A level isn't "good" or "bad." Anger can pull you out of fear. Pure bliss can be the wrong tool if you need to ship something.

Your energy level has a baseline, but it fluctuates due to events and actions. A weekend on the couch in front of the TV with accompanying snacks might temporarily bring someone in the 400s down into the 200s. That doesn't mean watching TV is "bad" — for someone at lower energy levels, TV is actually a way to increase their state. TV is great at creating Desire, which is a step up from the Shame, Apathy, and Guilt of those below 100.

Why This Book?

It's fascinating. I don't think it's possible to read through this book without discovering something new about yourself, or the people and environment around you. Throughout my life I have been through a lot of the different states Dodson talks about, and it's interesting to see how well my experience fits with his description of the energy levels — and how one ascends to the next level or descends.

"If you want to check whether you have been indoctrinated, notice how you react to disagreement with your world views. The stronger your emotional reaction to disagreement, the more likely it is that your views are a result of indoctrination rather than education."

What Stands Out

I love when I get my hands on a book like this that gives me a new lens through which to see the world. Every energy level gets its own detailed breakdown — and Dodson doesn't just give you abstract descriptions. He maps real behaviors, real situations, real people you'll recognize.

Assuming mask identities can drain a lot of energy. The best thing you can do to improve your state is to stop caring what others think about you. You can save an enormous amount of time and energy by releasing the need for approval.

Relax fully and work fully. Most people think about work when they're relaxing and about relaxing when working — turning life into a giant guilt trip. Master this.

Resistance draws towards us what we resist. Water naturally reaches its goal because it resists nothing. Resisting something will not solve it. It feeds it.

You experience the world, not as it is, but as you are. More energy for creativity is available at the higher levels. The lower your energy, the stronger your urge to "get" — there is a lot of power in releasing neediness.

"Moving only a few points up the scale can have enormous consequences on your experience of reality. It might render everything you held true and dear untrue and meaningless. It might provide a new lens through which to view things."

Moving Up the Scale

How long does it take to move up? It depends on whether a state is temporary or habitual. Many who start to focus on their spiritual development can see big changes fast — like moving 200 points up the scale. But big jumps like this are often temporary, and you easily slide back to your habitual level.

That doesn't mean it's a waste of time. Even if you temporarily spend time in a higher level, your base level will increase a bit. Temporarily raising from 200 to 400, you will most likely add 20 to your baseline from that experience. Moving up too fast is like jumping on a rapidly moving train — you're more likely to be hurt than enlightened. Move up step by step.

And there's a sticky factor: spending three weeks in a low state compared to your baseline makes it start to manifest around you. Your environment begins to match your energy.

The Disclaimer

The key to enjoying this book is to not expect to resonate with or believe everything it says. It talks about astral beings, demonic entities, and things that will push your skepticism to its limits. That's also what open-mindedness is for me — to be able to listen to arguments and ideas that go against your beliefs, then experiment with any new and seemingly useful information and see what works, and change views accordingly.

You don't have to believe the content and you still get huge benefits from reading it. Which is really cool.

💡 Key Takeaway

Knowing the scale alone will help your spiritual growth and expand your awareness. The scale represents a way to relate to the world. Confusion calms when the brain can categorize events — without judgment. To communicate effectively, it's important to know what kind of energies you're dealing with, because each level demands a different approach. And the most powerful move? Release the wants and the don't wants. Accept life as it is. Practice feeling happy today instead of projecting your happiness into the future.

⚖️ Verdict

Sometimes a book can be both the best and the worst book at the same time — depending on how cynical and scientific you want to be. This was the most interesting book I've read. It's super weird, but I think you can handle it! A very subjective pick, but for me it has a place on The Great Books List. If you're into Spiral Dynamics or Dodson's Parallel Universes of Self, this is essential reading.
