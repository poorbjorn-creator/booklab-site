Why the page matters more than the blast

Launch week is loud and short. The useful part is what still works in month three and month six.

  • Monthly releases pages bring in about a third of every Google click the site has ever had. They go live about a month early, so they can start ranking before pub day. The November 2026 list was on Google's first page a month before November began.
  • Spotlight pages keep drawing search traffic after the posts have scrolled by. The Process by Mitesh Jani is the site's second-best page, still ranking months after it went up. You're Not Dead Yet reached Google's first page within days.
  • A short timed to a release can show up in Google's video carousel. The one for The Infinity Machine did more than 1,600 views and sat at #1–2 for the title.

That's what you're buying. A page that stays findable, next to books I already recommend.
